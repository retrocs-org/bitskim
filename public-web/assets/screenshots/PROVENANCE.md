Images are cropped to the application window or text interface. Window borders belonging to other applications, surrounding desktops and capture controls are excluded. Cropping does not alter the application controls, status or text. The appearance progress bar is the application's labelled theme preview.

Screenshots illustrate the interface; they do not establish untested platform or hardware compatibility. See the release validation notes for current coverage.
