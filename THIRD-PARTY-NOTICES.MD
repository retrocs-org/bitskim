The RISC OS builds statically link UnixLib and GCC's libgcc from GCCSDK tag release_2_95_4_v3 (https://github.com/riscos-dot-info/gccsdk/tree/release_2_95_4_v3). UnixLib includes code by Simon Callan, Nick Burrett, Nicholas Clark, Peter Burwood, the University of California, Berkeley, Sun Microsystems and other contributors. Its complete supplied notices and licenses are in assets/licenses/UNIXLIB-COPYRIGHT and UNIXLIB-LGPL. GCC's GPL and libgcc linking-exception notice are in assets/licenses/GCC-GPL and LIBGCC-NOTICE. These third-party components are not relicensed under BitSkim's MIT license.

This product includes software developed by the University of California, Berkeley and its contributors.

Each RISC OS binary package supplies the original SDK source archive, the project's full source archive, host adaptation patch, four relinkable application objects and license texts. These permit rebuilding/relinking with a modified UnixLib. See docs/RISCOS.md. The source archive for BitSkim itself contains the application sources, patch and notices; the separate SDK source is included in each RISC OS binary distribution.

third_party/mtcp/REVISION. mTCP is GPL-3.0-or-later; its full source and licence

combined DOS executable is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. DOS packages
